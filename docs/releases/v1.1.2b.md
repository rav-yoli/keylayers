## KeyLayers 1.1.2b

### Keybindings

- **Assign keybindings** is now a tab in the main KeyLayers window. It enters Edit mode automatically, and the window can still be dragged by its header.
- See the entire Base group at once or focus on one bar. Generate Shift, Ctrl, or Alt shortcuts for configured frames in one click and review them before applying. Keep Alt for self-cast if you use it to cast on yourself.
- While assigning keybindings, hover over a shortcut in the tab or on your action bar to show the matching button with a red halo.

### Display and feedback

- Enable **Show keybindings only on active frames** in Settings to hide key labels on inactive KeyLayers action bars. The active bars show the key alone, such as **4** for **Shift+4**. The frame's learning label can show the modifier.
- Read what's new in the **Release notes** tab. **Report an issue** gives you the link to the project's [CurseForge issue tracker](https://legacy.curseforge.com/wow/addons/keylayers-by-rav-yoli/issues).
