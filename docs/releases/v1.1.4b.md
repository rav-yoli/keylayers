## KeyLayers 1.1.4b

- Assign a **Dedicated shortcut** to each **Toggle** layer under **Triggers**. For example, F8 can lock **Ctrl + Shift**. Press the same shortcut again to unlock, or use **Return to neutral** to return to Base.
- Physical Ctrl, Shift, and Alt keep their Hold behavior. Holding them no longer locks a layer, and the experimental tap-or-hold option has been replaced by dedicated shortcuts.
- Existing frames keep their mode and layer. Assign a shortcut to each existing Toggle layer you want to lock. You can also manage these shortcuts under WoW’s **Options → Keybindings → KeyLayers**.
