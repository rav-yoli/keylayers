# KeyLayers 2.1.11b

This beta release brings the full 2.1 development cycle together.

## Build your own action bars

Create **up to 12 KeyLayers bars**, with **up to 36 buttons per action bar**, and arrange them across rows and columns. Use KeyLayers as your action bar manager or keep using Blizzard bars or Bartender4. Assign shortcuts by hovering or with the binding assistant, and customize button styles and cooldown displays.

## Arrange frames and bars in a live editor

- **Containers** shows which bars belong to each frame. Create a frame and attach its first KeyLayers bar immediately. Move frames and bars in game with live coordinates. A lone bar carries its frame with it. Frames with several bars resize around their bars as you drag.
- **Inherited styles** lets you apply shared settings to frames or bars, refine them by type, and override them per item. Preview backgrounds, borders, and inherited changes directly in game.
- **Auto fit** adds or removes rows and columns when you resize a single-bar frame, within the 36-button limit. Turning it off keeps the current grid. Horizontal and vertical padding can be adjusted, linked, copied from another frame, or constrained by minimum values.

## Switch shaman totems on the fly

Save totem profiles and open a vertical carousel by holding an element's shortcut or right-clicking it. Scroll to select a totem, then tap the shortcut to cast it. Track active durations and dismiss individual totems. **Call of the Elements** follows the active KeyLayers profile.
